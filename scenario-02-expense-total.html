Keep in mind

Some spreadsheet programs read $89.99 as money and would count it. This lab's tool treats it as text, which is why it was skipped. awk adds with ordinary floating-point numbers and rounds the display to two decimals. For real money, an exact-decimal check like the Python one is safer. Dollar signs, thousand separators and refunds are common reasons real totals drift. Duplicates need evidence such as a receipt number. A matching total is a good sign, not an audit.
