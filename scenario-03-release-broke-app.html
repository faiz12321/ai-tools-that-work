The release broke the app

Yesterday's update made totals go negative. Compare the working version with the broken one, undo only the damage, and add a test.

Your brief

A tiny fictional shop calculator went from v1.0 to v1.1. Customers now see negative totals. The release has three changes, and not all of them are bad. Find the one that is, recover without losing the good work, and make sure it cannot come back.
